SyncGuard

SyncGuard is a Shopify app that checks whether every Shopify order has a matching record in your accounting system, and emails you when one is missing.

How it works

  • It reads your Shopify orders through the Shopify Admin API. From each order it takes only four fields: the order number, the date the order was created, the total amount, and the currency.
  • It deliberately requests no customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses.
  • It reads invoices and sales receipts from the accounting system you connect.
  • It compares the Shopify orders against those invoices and sales receipts.
  • It emails you when a Shopify order has no matching accounting record.

What it does not do

  • It never writes to your accounting system. It only reads.
  • It does not request or store customer contact details or addresses.

Data retention

SyncGuard stores only what is needed to perform the comparison, and deletes anything older than 90 days.
