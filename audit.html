Find the Shopify orders missing from your QuickBooks books

A Shopify to QuickBooks connector can report a successful sync while individual orders never actually reach QuickBooks. The connector says everything is fine. Some orders are still missing on the QuickBooks side.

How the audit works

  1. You or your bookkeeper send two exports covering the same period: a Shopify orders export and a QuickBooks sales transactions export.
  2. Before sending either file, remove customer names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses.
  3. We compare the two files.
  4. You get back three lists: every order missing from QuickBooks, every order matched, and every order where the order number matched but the amount did not.

What we never ask for

We never ask for passwords, logins or account access. Both files are deleted once the results are sent.

Price

250 US dollars for a full year of orders on one store. The first five audits are 100 dollars, because we want real results to point to.

What this does not cover

This only works for connectors that post individual orders to QuickBooks. It does not work for connectors that post payout summaries instead, such as A2X.

For bookkeepers

If your firm has several Shopify clients on QuickBooks Online, send the stores one at a time.
