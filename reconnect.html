Reconnecting SyncGuard

This page is a placeholder that explains how a merchant reconnects SyncGuard after disconnecting it.

Reconnect to Shopify

  1. Open the SyncGuard listing on the Shopify App Store, or in your Shopify admin go to Settings → Apps and sales channels → Shopify App Store and search for SyncGuard.
  2. Click Install.
  3. Approve the Shopify Admin API access the app requests. SyncGuard asks only for read access to your orders.

Reconnect your accounting system

When prompted, sign in to your accounting system and authorise SyncGuard again so it can read your invoices and sales receipts.

After reconnecting

Once both connections are in place, SyncGuard resumes comparing your Shopify orders with your accounting records and emailing you about any order that has no matching accounting record.
